Why Open Innovation & Fine-Tuning Matter Here

1. General Models Guess; Specialization Knows

Zero-shot proprietary models tend to hallucinate or flag innocuous phrases as "unfair" while missing binding arbitration waivers buried in warranty sections. By fine-tuning Qwen 4B on the expert-annotated LexGLUE UNFAIR-ToS benchmark, the weights encode exact legal precedent (CLAUDETTE project standards).

2. Provable, Measured Improvement

Instead of claiming "AI magic", we published a frozen evaluation comparing the zero-shot base model against our Tinker LoRA checkpoint. Every metric, precision score, and training loss curve is auditable on our Evaluation Dashboard.
