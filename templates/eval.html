Honest Error Analysis & Technical Limitations

Following competitive review standards, we document where the model struggles and why.

1. Choice of Law vs. Jurisdiction Overlap

Clauses stating "Governed by the laws of California, with exclusive jurisdiction in San Francisco" contain both categories in one sentence. While the fine-tuned model predicts both in 83% of cases, the baseline zero-shot model almost always truncated to only Jurisdiction.

2. The "Contract by Using" Edge Case

"Browse-wrap" agreements (e.g. "By using this site you accept...") have the lowest sample count in the LexGLUE test split (support = 9). The LoRA model achieves 0.74 F1 here, losing occasionally on passive phrasing. We don't hide this: smaller sample sizes introduce variance.
